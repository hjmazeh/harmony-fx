Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a draft policy pending legal review. It describes, in good faith, what data Harmony currently collects and how it is used — it has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel and should not be relied on as a final compliance document until it has been.

This Privacy Policy explains how Harmony ("Harmony", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website (harmonyfx.me) and client portal (the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

Account information you provide when you sign up: full name, phone number, email address, and password (or your Google account information if you sign up with Google).

Identity verification (KYC) information we collect once you begin trading account verification: date of birth, nationality, city and country of residence, a government-issued ID (front and back), and a selfie photo for identity confirmation. This information is required to activate a live trading account and is a standard requirement across the financial services industry.

Trading account information: your MT5 login and server details, and a record of account activity relevant to servicing your account.

Financial transaction information: deposit and withdrawal requests, the amounts involved, the payment method used (e.g. Whish Money, USDT, or other supported methods), destination details you provide for withdrawals (such as a phone number or wallet address), and receipt images you upload to confirm a deposit.

Referral information: your unique referral code and, if you were referred by another client, the referral code used.

Communications: messages you send us through WhatsApp, email, or the support channels linked from the portal.

Technical information: basic information stored in your browser (such as your language preference) to make the portal work correctly across visits.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and trading account.
  • To verify your identity, as required before activating a live trading account.
  • To process deposit and withdrawal requests and communicate their status to you.
  • To operate the referral program, including calculating and paying referral commissions.
  • To respond to support requests and important account notifications.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary to provide the Services:

  • With payment and cash-handling partners we work with, solely to process your deposits and withdrawals.
  • With email service providers, solely to deliver account and transaction notifications to you.
  • With our infrastructure providers (such as Google Firebase), which host our systems and data.
  • Where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including access controls restricting who at Harmony can view your data, and secure cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to meet legal, tax, and regulatory record-keeping obligations typical in the financial services industry.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below, subject to our legal obligation to retain certain records (for example, KYC records) for a required period even after a deletion request.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember convenience settings such as your preferred language and dismissed notifications. This information stays on your own device and is not used for advertising or tracking across other websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to, and may not be used by, anyone under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us
